Uniformity

A 43-page design document for a stylised third-person RPG set in a city divided into four districts, each with its own aesthetic and cultural identity.

NarrativeWorldDocs

What it is

The city is the system. Four districts, four cultures, and a player who has to move between them — so the document covers art direction, narrative structure, progression, combat and NPC design as one connected whole rather than separate chapters that happen to share a setting.
