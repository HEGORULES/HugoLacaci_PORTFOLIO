Custom TFT Set

A full Teamfight Tactics set built from zero on my own time — champions, costs, 16 traits, and a damage model to balance the numbers against.

SystemsEconomyBalance

What it is

Nobody asked for this one. I wanted to find out whether I could hold an entire competitive economy in my head: champions across five cost tiers, 16 traits that have to combine without collapsing into one dominant line, and item interactions on top. The trait sheet runs 3 tabs; the damage calculator runs 5, covering champions, base items, items and the calculator itself.

My role

Solo — design, documentation and the whole balancing model.
