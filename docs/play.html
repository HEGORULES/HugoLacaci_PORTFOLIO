Prototype: Automatic Battle

A two-player, turn-based-into-real-time lane battler built in under a week. Each round both players spend a shared gold budget to place Soldiers, Tanks, Rangers and Mages across three lanes, then watch them clash automatically. Win two lanes to take the round, two rounds to take the match, so the whole game rests on those four units feeling fair and distinct.
